Why "85% fill" is measured on the longest edge here
Amazon's guidance says the product should occupy 85% of the frame without defining area versus linear measure. Area is the stricter reading — but a tall narrow product (a bottle, a pen) can never reach 85% of a square frame's area no matter how you crop it, so an area-based fixer would loop forever. Enforcement in practice tracks the longest dimension, so that's what the repair targets. The audit reports both numbers so you can see exactly where you stand.
